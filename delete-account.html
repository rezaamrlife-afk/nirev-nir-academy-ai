Delete Your Account

NIR Academy · NIREV AI

You can request deletion of your NIREV AI account and all associated data at any time. Please follow the steps below.

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open the NIREV AI app or visit nirev-nir-academy-ai.vercel.app
  2. Sign in to your account
  3. Go to Settings from the sidebar
  4. Scroll down to the Account section
  5. Send a deletion request via email to info@niracademy.com with the subject: "Delete My Account"

What Data Will Be Deleted

Upon account deletion, the following data will be permanently removed within 30 days:

  1. Your account profile (name, email, CEFR level, goals)
  2. All assessment history and responses
  3. AI-generated scores, feedback, and reports
  4. Progress snapshots and analytics data
  5. All personal preferences and settings

What Data May Be Retained

Aggregated and fully anonymized system logs may be retained for up to 90 days for security and fraud prevention purposes. These logs contain no personally identifiable information.
