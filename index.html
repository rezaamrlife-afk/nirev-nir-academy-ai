How It Works

From sign-up to insight in four steps

  1. Create your account

    Sign up with your email and choose whether you are a Learner or a Teacher.

  2. Take an assessment

    Pick an assessment from the Assessment page and complete it.

  3. Get AI-enhanced feedback

    Receive your score breakdown and feedback right after you finish.

  4. Track and review

    Follow progress, explore analytics and open your reports over time.
