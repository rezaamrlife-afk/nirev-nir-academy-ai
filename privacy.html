Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

NIREV AI ("we", "our", or "us") is an AI-powered English language assessment platform developed by NIR Academy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal data when you use our services.

Data Controller: NIR Academy is the data controller for all personal data processed through NIREV AI. Contact: info@niracademy.com

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Password (securely hashed via Supabase Auth — never stored in plain text)

1.2 Profile Information

  • CEFR level
  • Learning goals
  • User role (learner or teacher)

1.3 Assessment Data

  • User responses to tasks and questions
  • AI-generated scores and feedback
  • Progress history and performance metrics

1.4 Usage Data

  • Feature interactions and session activity
  • Browser type and device category (non-identifying)
  • Timestamps of key actions

We do not collect IP addresses for tracking or advertising purposes.

2. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal data based on the following legal grounds:

  • Contract performance — to deliver the NIREV AI service you signed up for
  • Legitimate interests — for security, fraud prevention, and platform improvement
  • Consent — where explicitly requested (e.g. optional communications)

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Provide AI-based English language assessments
  • Generate personalized feedback and scoring
  • Track learning progress over time
  • Improve platform performance and features
  • Maintain account security and authentication
  • Send essential service notifications

4. AI Processing

NIREV AI uses Groq as an AI infrastructure provider to process assessment responses and generate results in real time.

  • Groq acts as a data processor on our behalf — not as an independent data controller
  • Your assessment input is transmitted to Groq transiently for real-time processing only
  • Groq does not use your data for model training or independent purposes
  • We do not use your data to train any AI models

Groq's processing is governed by their Data Processing Agreement and privacy policies.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored using Supabase (EU-based infrastructure, Frankfurt). We implement:

  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Row Level Security (RLS) on all database tables
  • Secure authentication and session management
  • No sale of personal data to any third party

6. Third-Party Services

  • Supabase — authentication and database hosting (EU)
  • Groq — AI processing (data processor only)
  • Vercel — application hosting and deployment

Each provider processes data under their own security policies and Data Processing Agreements.

7. Data Retention

  • Active accounts: data is retained while the account exists
  • Deleted accounts: all personal data is permanently removed within 30 days
  • System logs: aggregated or anonymized system logs may be retained for up to 90 days for security, debugging, and abuse prevention

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Export your assessment history
  • Object to or restrict certain types of processing
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

Submit requests to: info@niracademy.com. We aim to respond within 30 days.

9. Cookies and Tracking

We use only essential cookies and local storage for:

  • Authentication sessions
  • User preferences

We do not use advertising cookies, third-party trackers, or behavioral analytics.

10. Children's Privacy

NIREV AI is intended for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided personal data, we will delete it promptly.

11. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed in jurisdictions outside your country of residence (including the EU and US). Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) to ensure your data is protected in accordance with applicable law.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Significant changes will be communicated via email or in-app notification at least 14 days before taking effect. Continued use of the platform after that date indicates acceptance of the updated policy.
