Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

NIREV AI ("we", "our", or "us") is an AI-powered English language assessment platform developed by NIR Academy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you use NIREV AI:

  • Account information: Name, email address, and password (stored securely via Supabase)
  • Profile information: CEFR level, learning goals, and role (learner/teacher)
  • Assessment data: Your answers, scores, and feedback from assessments
  • Usage data: Pages visited, features used, and session information

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide personalized English language assessments
  • Generate AI-powered scores, feedback, and predictions
  • Track your progress and learning journey
  • Improve our platform and features
  • Send important account notifications

3. AI Processing

NIREV AI uses the Groq API (powered by Llama AI models) to generate assessment questions, score your responses, and provide feedback. Your assessment answers are sent to Groq's servers for processing. Groq's privacy policy applies to this processing. We do not use your data to train AI models.

4. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, hosted in the EU (Frankfurt, Germany). We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS)
  • Row-level security on all database tables
  • Secure authentication via Supabase Auth

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with:

  • Supabase: Database and authentication services
  • Groq: AI processing for assessment generation and scoring
  • Vercel: Application hosting

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Export your assessment history

7. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, all personal data is permanently removed within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

NIREV AI is designed for English language learners and teachers aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

9. Cookies

We use essential cookies and local storage to maintain your session and remember your preferences. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via email or in-app notification. Continued use of NIREV AI after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
