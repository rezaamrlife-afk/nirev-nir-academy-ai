Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

NIREV AI ("we", "our", or "us") is an AI-powered English language assessment platform developed by NIR Academy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal data when you use our services.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Password (securely hashed via Supabase Auth)

1.2 Profile Information

  • CEFR level
  • Learning goals
  • User role (learner or teacher)

1.3 Assessment Data

  • User responses to tasks and questions
  • AI-generated scores and feedback
  • Progress history and performance metrics

1.4 Usage Data

  • Feature interactions
  • Session activity
  • Basic device and log information (non-advertising)

2. How We Use Your Data

  • Provide AI-based English assessments
  • Generate personalized feedback and scoring
  • Track learning progress over time
  • Improve platform performance and features
  • Maintain account security and authentication
  • Send essential service notifications

3. AI Processing

NIREV AI uses third-party AI infrastructure provided by Groq to process assessment responses and generate results. When you submit answers:

  • Your input is temporarily transmitted to Groq for processing
  • Responses are generated in real time
  • We do not use your data to train AI models

Data processing by Groq is subject to their own privacy and retention policies.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase (EU-based infrastructure). We implement:

  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Row Level Security (RLS) on all database tables
  • Secure authentication and session management

We do not sell personal data.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Supabase — authentication and database hosting
  • Groq — AI processing for assessments
  • Vercel — application hosting and deployment

6. Data Retention

  • Active accounts: data is retained while the account exists
  • Deleted accounts: all personal data is removed within 30 days
  • Some anonymized logs may be retained for security and system integrity

7. Your Rights

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Export your assessment history

Requests can be submitted via: info@niracademy.com

8. Cookies and Tracking

We use only essential cookies and local storage for authentication sessions and user preferences. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party behavioral tracking.

9. Children's Privacy

NIREV AI is intended for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If such data is identified, it will be deleted promptly.

10. International Users

Your data may be processed in jurisdictions outside your country of residence. We ensure appropriate safeguards for international data transfers.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Significant changes will be communicated via email or in-app notification. Continued use of the platform indicates acceptance of updates.
